## Overview

Forever Clothing uses a restrained editorial fashion register for shoppers who want an easy, confident way to browse clothes. The home page is a crisp white gallery around an ink-blue, oversized FOREVER wordmark and a single model image. That wordmark-and-figure overlap is the signature; everything else stays quiet.

## Colors

Ink gives headings and actions a tailored, almost-black blue. Paper is reserved for the hero field; white is the surrounding storefront canvas. Rules and muted text support navigation without competing with product imagery.

## Typography

Outfit is used with strong weight and tight tracking for brand statements, and with small uppercase labels for utility navigation. Copy is concise, direct, and never decorative.

## Layout

The storefront is centered at 1440px with 16px phone gutters. The phone hero is deliberately 35rem tall so customers see the next section sooner; desktop can breathe at 46rem. Controls maintain 44px minimum touch targets on phones.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are flat. Only the primary hero action carries a small, soft shadow to establish it as the next step.

## Shapes

Use square edges and fine rules. Avoid rounded cards and pill controls.

## Components

The header uses a centered lockup, quiet navigation, and recognisable icon actions. Hero actions are clear: Shop now is primary; Explore new in is secondary.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do give product images and editorial type space. Do keep motion short and respect reduced-motion preferences. Don't add gradients, decorative badges, or rounded UI that softens the editorial system.
